# Employee Attrition Analysis (IBM HR Dataset)

Dataset: IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance (Kaggle)

Records: 1,470 employees

Tools: SQL (SQLite), Power BI

## Key Findings

### 1. Attrition by department

- Sales: 20.6% (92 of 446)

- Human Resources: 19.0% (12 of 63)

- Research & Development: 13.8% (133 of 961)

### 2. Overtime

- With overtime: 30.5% (127 of 416)

- Without overtime: 10.4% (110 of 1,054)

- Employees working overtime left about 3 times more.

### 3. Monthly income

- Under 3,000: 28.6%

- 3,000-5,999: 12.7%

- 6,000-9,999: 12.0%

- 10,000+: 8.9%

### 4. Age and tenure

- Left: average age 33.6, average tenure 5.1 years

- Stayed: average age 37.6, average tenure 7.4 years

### Overall attrition rate: 16.1% (237 of 1,470)
